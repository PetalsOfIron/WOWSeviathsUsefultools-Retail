The Mount Collector Mounts
In Battle for Azeroth, there are two new milestones for mount collectors to achieve,  No Stable Big Enough for collecting 350 mounts and  A Horde of Hoofbeats for collecting 400 mounts. There have been enough mounts before Battle for Azeroth to achieve these early on, but Battle for Azeroth brings many new mounts to help you reach and obtain these mounts. So keep in mind that all the mounts below in this guide are also working towards these two new achievement mounts or even earlier ones in their series.

The last mount milestone achievement,  Lord of the Reins, was added back during Patch 6.2.2 of the Warlords of Draenor expansion.

Ground Mount
This mount is a reward from the achievement,  No Stable Big Enough, for collecting 350 mounts.

Things to know about obtaining this:
Only the total amount of mounts a single character can use will count towards this total.
Paladin and Warlock class-specific mounts will only count towards the maximum amount on those characters. This extra count, however, doesn't happen with Legion class mounts.
Gladiator mounts and certain other special mounts that are not account-wide will only count towards the maximum of the characters that earn these mounts.
Legion class mounts for an unknown reason count towards the maximum across your account.
Paladins have an advantage with three unique class specific mounts.
Strategy to Obtain This Mount
There are a tremendous amount of mounts, and it would be too much to go into detail about how to obtain all of them, but here are some general tips about increasing your mount collection count.
Have a Paladin. Simply put, Paladins have their two class-specific mounts and can also obtain an additional mount at the Argent Tournament that no other class can use.
Having multiple characters allows you to make multiple chances at mounts that a character normally only gets one chance per day/week.
Legion Class Mounts are special and share credit across your account. A guide to Legion Class mounts can be found here: Obtaining Legion Class Mounts.
Have fun! If you are just getting into mount collecting, it is a long journey, and you will have to collect many rare mounts that are worth being excited about obtaining after grinding them out.

Rare Drop
The following mounts are rare drops from certain Dungeons in Battle for Azeroth. These can only drop in the Mythic version of these dungeons, giving players one chance a week on a particular character. However these mounts are also available in the Mythic Keystone version of these dungeons and have a chance to be in the "Challenger's Cache" located at the end of a Mythic Keystone dungeon along with the other loot of the dungeon. Therefore multiple attempts can be made at these mounts provided you have a  Mythic Keystone for one of these dungeons. These mounts are awarded automatically through Personal Loot to a player that has not already learned the mount and is tradeable between members of the group.

The last time dungeon mount drops were implemented was in the Cataclysm expansion.

Ground Mount
This mount is a rare drop from the Unbound Abomination in Mythic The Underrot on a weekly lockout. This mount can also be contained in the "Challenger's Cache" at the end of a  Mythic Keystone The Underrot dungeon in addition to the other loot. Beating the timer and the level of the keystone does not affect the chance for this mount to drop.

Estimated Drop Chance: 1%

Things to know about obtaining this:
Awarded through Personal Loot.
The mount is tradeable between other group members in the dungeon.
A player who already has the mount learned will not be able to loot another from the boss or chest.
Obtaining the mount does not trigger a guild message like some other rare drop mounts.
Strategy to Obtain This Mount

This mount can be obtained in a very similar way as  Sharkbait's Favorite Crackers.

There is a good chance you may obtain this mount, or encounter someone that has it drop while doing regular Mythic and Mythic+ activities to gear up. If you are interested in getting this mount but aren't in a rush to collect it, it is smart not to go too crazy targeting Underrot Keystones as you will likely be doing plenty of Mythic+ Underrot throughout the entire expansion.

Now if you are eager to add this mount to your collection, there are two good ways to grind out attempts at this mount. In both cases, because the mount is personal loot and the chance to obtain is the same regardless of how many players are present, I suggest doing the following with a group of friends. Perhaps you can even convince a friend to trade you the mount if they get it!

The first method involves speed running regular Mythic The Underrot, skipping as many bosses and trash to get to the last boss, Unbound Abomination who drops the mount. Luckily, in this dungeon, you don't need to kill any other bosses to get to the last one. Therefore clear trash and walk past bosses until you get to the end. Unfortunately, regular Mythic dungeons have a weekly lockout, so after you kill the end boss once; you will either have to swap to a different character and clear again, or wait for the weekly reset. Overall this method is the fastest per attempt and regular Mythic will be easier to clear as you get more gear, but this is limited based on how many characters you have at level 120 and how geared the group is.

The second method involves targeting The Underrot  Mythic Keystones. The best way is to form a four-person group, preferably with a tank and healer already, and then list your group in the Group Finder and ask specifically for Underrot keystones. As the mount has an equal drop chance regardless of the level, pick any level keystone you prefer. If you are solely seeking the mount, lower level keystones will be faster. The advantage to this method is that you can grind out attempts at this mount with one character, but you won't be able to skip bosses or too much trash like you can on regular Mythic dungeons.

Strategy to Obtain This Mount
The following information applies to all 4 of the Kul'Tiras and Zandalar World Drop mounts so if you have read up on one of the other mounts; you may want to skip three paragraphs to the section specific about this mount.

The chance for this mount to drop is very very low and is going to take a significant amount of time and luck (or just extreme luck) to get one to drop. That being said, there are a couple of things to consider before heading out to Vol'dun to mindlessly grind for who knows how long.

Firstly, if you aren't in a rush to get this mount and have Horde characters to level, consider holding off this mount grind until after you have leveled all your Horde characters through Vol'dun. You may conveniently loot this mount while simply questing through the zone. While very unlikely, it will still feel much more efficient to accomplish other tasks while searching for this mount.

Second, the mount is "Bind on Use" meaning if you have gold to spend, you can check the Auction House or check out trade chat and buy the mount to learn for yourself.

Dune Scavenger Farm

Now if you want to get this mount and aren't going to pay-to-win this into your collection, then it is off to the dunes with you. I would highly suggest attempting to join or form a five man group to help tag and kill creatures faster than you could solo. Due to the mount being personal loot, you don't reduce your chance of getting a mount by being in a group. However, a raid group is a no-go, as many items including this mount will not drop for players in raid groups.

You'll be looking to kill the snake looking creatures of Vol'dun called Sethrak. Just about any creature with the word "Sethrak" or "Faithless" in their name. These creatures can be found all over Vol'dun so you have a lot of options and places you may visit to attempt to get this mount. Now popular areas to farm these Sethrak have a history of having spawn rates of creatures nerfed, but here is some information on good locations to farm.

This map marks several locations where you can find "Sethrak" or "Faithless" type creatures that can drop the  Captured Dune Scavenger.

Vol'dun

Hyper Spawns - This term is used to describe a method of farming a large number of creatures at a fast rate. Hyper Spawn farming is abusing a forced respawn on certain creatures in a reasonably small area, often NPCs that are killed as a requirement of a quest. Generally, these NPC's respawn timer is affected based on how often they are being killed to help quest pacing in crowded areas. So if many creatures in the area are being killed often, the respawn of these creatures will speed up to accommodate.

In some areas, this causes the respawn of NPCs to get as low as 10 seconds, allowing a group to set up camp in a small area and farm the same ten mobs about every 15 seconds. Many of these have been nerfed but is still something to keep an eye out for.

PET BATTLE INFORMATION / GUIDE INSTRUCTIONS
How to Use This Guide
Download an addon that uses waypoints, such as TomTom - Copy waypoints into your game to find suggested capture points for these battle pets. You can use an addon like Paste to copy a large amount of waypoints. If you do not use waypoints, follow the guidance in the 'Notes' section or click on the battle pet's name to be taken to a page with a map of pins showing where that pet has been caught.
Using the waypoints for all zones, make your way through each continent following the waypoints - Each section attempts to stay in order of zones you are flying through (with the exception of powerful pets at the top). As multiple waypoints per pet are provided, you can clear out the remaining waypoints for that pet once it has been found. If you are unable to find that battle pet at the waypoint provided, check the 'Notes' or click on the battle pet's name in the guide for more information on where to find that pet.
For "multiple continent" pets - Catch them as you see them, and make your way back through the zones if any are missed.
How to Capture a Wild Battle Pet
Wild pets are found roaming around nearly every zone in the game. Most zones have completely unique pets while also sharing some common pets between them.

To capture a wild battle pet, you must lower its health below the threshold decided by your traps— Trap,  Strong Trap,  Pristine Trap—then use that trap to capture them. If successful, the battle pet is added to your collection. You can then battle with it, level it, and show it off!

Some wild battle pets only spawn in specific weather or time conditions, such as Sandstorm, Night, Summer/Winter, Snow, or Rain. Keep an eye out on the notes for these pets.
Best Pets for Capturing Wild Pets
Unlike current Retail, there's probably only one pet that's especially good to catch wild pets with—Terrible Turnip. This is due to having the ability  Weakening Blow.

Other than this, bring your regular pet roster (preferably with some healing abilities) to capture wild battle pets—and plenty of  Battle Pet Bandage.
Differences Between Retail and Classic
There are two key differences between Retail Mists of Pandaria and Classic pet battles:
Availability of battle pets - Some key battle pets that were not introduced until later Mists of Pandaria patches are immediately available on either the launch or pre-patch of Mists of Pandaria. This includes pets such as Unborn Val'kyr, Emerald Proto-Whelp and Nexus Whelpling.
Unavailability of battle pets - Some battle pets that were added as part of events, or in the Blizzard store, are unavailable in Mists of Pandaria Classic. These were pets that were commonly used to capture battle pets, or to fight with. This includes pets such as Lil' Ragnaros. While overall not a big deal for capturing pets in general, your favourite pets from back then may not be available.
MOST POWERFUL WILD-CAUGHT BATTLE PETS
Eastern Kingdoms
Blighthawk - One of the best undead pets, Blighthawk allows for massive healing with  Consume Corpse.  Cyclone can be combined with other battle pet abilities that buff damage, to wreak havoc to backline pets.
Jade Oozeling - A few clones of this pet exist in the game, but this is one of the easier versions to obtain. This pet can put out large amounts of burst damage with  Expunge.
Kalimdor
Nether Faerie Dragon - This pet has a fantastic defensive ability with  Evanescence, as well as high-output damage abilities, like  Life Exchange,  Moonfire and  Arcane Blast—combined with being a  Dragonkin pet.
Nordrassil Wisp -  Flash is a great defensive ability and allows for high damage when combined with  Light.
Outland
Flayer Youngling - A speedy breed allows you to use  Kick to get ahead in the fight, damage-wise and move-wise, potentially interrupting or throwing off the opponent's abilities.  Blitz allows you to deal large damage when you attack first, and  Deflection allows you to completely negate an enemy ability.
Fel Flame -  Scorched Earth and  Conflagrate, when combined, allow you to do large amounts of damage to the enemy pet in succession.
Northrend
Unborn Val'kyr -  Curse of Doom can be used to line up with damage-increasing effects, and  Haunt /  Unholy Ascension are used to easily swap out to a different pet while still applying damage.
Nexus Whelpling -  Arcane Storm and  Mana Surge can be used to deal massive amounts of damage, especially in combination with the Dragonkin family ability,  Dragonkin.
Emerald Proto-Whelp -  Emerald Presence can be used to negate a lot of damage from enemy pets, combined with the flexibility of  Emerald Dream allowing for more sustain, and  Proto-Strike for more damage and even more sustain if needed. This pet is also a  Dragonkin, allowing for powerful attacks.
Multiple / Other Continents
Frogs / Toads (eg., Toad, Small Frog) -  Water Jet,  Healing Wave and  Frog Kiss can be used to deal good damage while keeping your pet alive.
Rabbits / Hares (eg., Rabbit, Alpine Hare) - A S/S rabbit can be very powerful with  Flurry,  Dodge and  Burrow, making it the best Critter pet in the game. You can avoid many abilities while dealing high damage.
Moths (eg., Oasis Moth, Grey Moth) - A moth with  Alpha Strike,  Cocoon Strike and  Moth Dust can deal high damage while avoiding abilities from the enemy pet.
Crow -  Call Darkness and  Nocturnal Strike synergize with each other allowing high damage output.
